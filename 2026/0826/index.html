Chinese Jokes in the New Era

August 26, 2026 Humor views:

People Really Do Laugh in Hell

1: An Old Man Calls for Help

An old man fell into a river and shouted for help, but the police ignored him. The old man changed his tune and shouted,“Down with the Communist Party!”Two police officers immediately jumped in, pulled him out, then gagged him and handcuffed him.

2: A Call from the Commission for Discipline Inspection

The Commission for Discipline Inspection called all bureau chiefs to say,“Please come to the Commission tomorrow.”The result: the Land and Resources Bureau chief suffered a heart attack; the Finance Bureau chief turned himself in that very night; the Transportation Bureau chief fled to Canada; and the Health Bureau chief committed suicide by poisoning. A young Commission member sighed,“I just said one simple thing.”

3: An Official’s Son Visits in Prison

After a certain official was placed under“shuanggui”(double regulation), his son visited him in prison and cried,“Dad, I just graduated from college and can’t find a job…” The official wrote a note to his son saying,“Take this to my former subordinates.”The son worried,“What good is writing a note now?! The official patted his chest and assured him,“Don’t worry. When I was in power, I could make whoever I wanted an official. Now that I’m inside, I can make whoever I want come in here.”

4: Differences Between Chinese and Foreign Fairy Tales

Foreign fairy tales usually begin with“Once upon a time,”while Chinese fairy tales start with:“By the end of 2030, within the next five years, or thirty years from now.”

5: Mineral Reserves

Antimony is a metal with the chemical symbol Sb (which sounds like the Chinese slang term for“idiot”: Shabi). China is one of the earliest countries in the world to have discovered and utilized antimony. China accounts for more than half of the world’s currently proven antimony reserves. After the founding of the People’s Republic of China, large-scale exploration and development of antimony took place. Currently, China ranks first in the world in both antimony reserves and production.

6: Flag-Raising at the Temple

After the founding of the People’s Republic of China, an elderly monk led the monks at his Buddhist temple in a national flag-raising ceremony for the first time. A young novice monk, unfamiliar with the practice, asked the elderly monk,“Why do we need to raise the flag every day before eating vegetarian meals and chanting the Buddha’s name?”The elderly monk replied solemnly,“Because without the Communist Party, there would be no Tathāgata—the infinitely holy Buddha of great compassion and mercy, Communism!”
